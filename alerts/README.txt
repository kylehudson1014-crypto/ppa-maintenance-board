Free email alerts for the PPA Maintenance Board (Google Apps Script). Full click-by-click steps are in the setup Google Doc.
Files to copy into a new Apps Script project: Code.gs (this folder) + js/status.js (rename to status.gs).
Sending stays OFF until the board's Settings > Alerts > "Send alert emails" switch is turned ON by an editor.
Gmail consumer quota: about 100 recipients/day for Apps Script MailApp.
